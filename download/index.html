What the app does

It's made for one job: seeing your orders quickly. Prices, deals and sales charts stay in the full dashboard.

🔔

New orders, live

Website orders appear instantly with a sound, a vibration and a banner.

📋

Full details

Customer name, phone, address, notes, every item and the total.

📞

One-tap contact

Call the customer or open WhatsApp straight from the order.

🗂️

Order history

Search by name, phone or dish, with daily totals for the last 7 days, 30 days or all time.

How to install

Log in with your admin email and password. Only that account can see orders.

Android phone

  1. Download the appTap the button below. Your phone may warn that this type of file can be harmful; tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the fileTap the download notification, or find cynthia-orders.apk in your Downloads.
  3. Allow the installIf asked, turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Cynthia Orders and log inThe app icon appears on your home screen.
